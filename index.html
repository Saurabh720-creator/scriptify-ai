SOP For Clients

Project Workflow

Scriptify AI follows a structured multilingual workflow:

  • Transcription → Translation → Final Review
  • Maintain original meaning, tone, and context
  • Deliver clean, natural, and high-quality output
  • Ensure AI model training compatibility

Translation Standards

  • Focus on meaning rather than literal word translation
  • Keep conversational flow natural and culturally accurate
  • Avoid unnecessary fillers like “um” and “ah”
  • Maintain clarity and readability across languages

Slang & Jargon Handling

  • Do not translate slang word-for-word
  • Use culturally appropriate equivalents
  • Preserve conversational tone and intent
  • Technical jargon such as API, NLP, Backend, ASR must be preserved accurately

PII Protection

  • All personal information is protected using [PII] tags
  • Names, addresses, phone numbers, and IDs are masked
  • Strict confidentiality and compliance standards are followed
